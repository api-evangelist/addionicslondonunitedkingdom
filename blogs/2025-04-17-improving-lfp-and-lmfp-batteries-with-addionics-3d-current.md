LFP batteries have been adapted by replacing some of the iron used as the cathode material with manganese, LMFP batteries emerging as an evolution of LFP, offering improved performance in specific areas. Advancements in battery design, including structural innovations such as 3D current collectors, can further enhance their performance, efficiency, and commercial viability.
